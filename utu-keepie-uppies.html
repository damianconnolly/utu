UTU Keepie Uppies

Welcome to UTU Keepie Uppies, a development group designed for primary school aged children (4-11) in Upminster to learn football skills and have fun. We offer a supportive environment where every child is encouraged to grow at their own pace.

Our sessions are structured by experienced and enthusiastic youth coaches, ensuring they are fun, educational, and provide a fantastic chance to make great friends. Our coaches use a variety of games and challenges to ensure young footballers fall in love with the game, learning skills and preparing themselves to confidently take part in football matches.

As children develop their skills with Keepie Uppies, there may be opportunities to join existing Upminster Town United teams, or to form new ones from our Keepie Uppies cohorts.

Keepie Uppie sessions will be starting this term - these will run on Saturday mornings in Upminster throughout school term time. We are currently gathering details of people who may be interested in joining our group. Please fill out the form below to register your interest.
